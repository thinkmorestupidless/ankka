# Glossary

The words this project's features use, each in exactly one sense.

## The project

### {{project}}
The project: the services below, deployed each on its own, which deliver what is required of them
when they run together.

### {{first}}
The service that owns items as people add to them.

### {{second}}
The service that records what {{first}} says of its items.

### item
A thing with a name and a count, kept by {{first}}.

### crossing
What passes between two of the project's services: a declared topic with its contract, or a route
of an endpoint.

### contract
The name and schema of what a declared topic carries, such as `{{first}}.v1`.

## Everyday words

add, count, name, record
