# Glossary

The words the research digest's features use, each in exactly one sense.

### research digest
The sample service: a watch that searches literature sources every day and keeps an entry per
paper, and a digest that writes a script every week from the entries found in its period.

### watch
The research digest's daily blueprint, which searches its sources and keeps an entry per paper.

### digest
The research digest's weekly blueprint, which writes a script from the entries found in its
period.

### source
Where the watch searches for papers: a literature index or preprint server.

### paper
A piece of published research, known by its identifier however many sources find it.

Avoid: article, publication

### entry
What the watch keeps for one paper: its identifier, the sources that found it and when it was
first found.

### script
What the digest writes for someone to listen to: statements, each naming a paper.

Avoid: transcript, episode

### statement
One thing a script says.

### period
The week a run of the digest covers, from one Sunday evening to the next.
